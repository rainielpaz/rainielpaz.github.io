  1. 01 · INGEST

    Organize and sync

    Footage is sorted, labeled and synced with audio so nothing gets lost and the edit starts clean.

  2. 02 · ROUGH CUT

    Find the story

    I pull the strongest moments and lay down the structure, the hook and the overall flow.

  3. 03 · FINE CUT

    Tighten the pacing

    Every cut is trimmed to the frame. Titles, graphics and b-roll are layered in where they add meaning.

  4. 04 · COLOR

    Grade the look

    Shots are balanced to match, then graded so the whole piece has one consistent mood.

  5. 05 · SOUND

    Mix the audio

    Dialogue is cleaned and leveled, with music and sound effects mixed to support the picture.

  6. 06 · DELIVER

    Export for the platform

    Final files are rendered in the right format, aspect ratio and size for wherever they will play.

I'm Rainiel Paz, a video editor with a background in computer engineering. I bring the same habits to editing that engineering taught me: a clear structure, careful attention to detail and a workflow that runs smoothly from start to finish.

Most of my work is short-form advertising: DTC product ads with multiple hook variations, animation style ads, talking heads, AI avatar shorts and VSLs. Good editing is mostly invisible. The viewer should feel the rhythm without noticing the cuts.

If you have footage that needs a story, take a look at my work and let's talk about your next project.
