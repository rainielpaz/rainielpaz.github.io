How an edit comes together

Every project moves through the same six stages, from the first import to the file you publish.

  1. 01

    Organize and sync

    Footage is sorted, labeled and synced with audio so the edit starts clean.

  2. 02

    Find the hook

    I pull the strongest moments and build the structure around the first three seconds.

  3. 03

    Tighten the pacing

    Every cut is trimmed to the frame. Titles, captions and b-roll go in where they add meaning.

  4. 04

    Grade the look

    Shots are matched, then graded so the whole ad has one consistent mood.

  5. 05

    Mix the sound

    Dialogue is cleaned and leveled, with music and effects mixed to support the picture.

  6. 06

    Export for the platform

    Final files go out in the right format, aspect ratio and size for where they will run.

About

An engineer in the edit bay

I'm Rainiel Paz, a video editor with a degree in computer engineering. I bring an engineer's habits to the timeline: clear structure, close attention to detail and a workflow that runs the same way every time.

Most of my work is short-form advertising: DTC product ads with several hook variations, animation style ads, talking heads, AI avatar shorts and VSLs. Good editing is mostly invisible. The viewer should feel the rhythm without noticing the cuts.
